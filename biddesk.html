Government Bid Desk

Public-sector bids, matched to your company

We help small IT, managed services, and facilities firms in Los Angeles, Ventura, and Kern counties find government bids that fit, and get proposals ready to submit.

What we do

We scan federal bids on SAM.gov, state bids on Cal eProcure, and local city, county, school, and special-district bid sites. When a bid looks like a fit for your company, we flag it, help you weigh a go or no-go, and help prepare the proposal — the compliance matrix, the narrative, and the forms.

You set the pricing, sign, and submit. We stay in a support role.

How we can help

📋

Matched bid list

A short list of open bids that look like a fit for your work and the counties you serve. You can request a free list below.

🔍

Bid vetting

Help reviewing a bid so you can decide whether to go or pass.

📝

Bid preparation

Help with the compliance matrix, narrative, and forms. You set the price, sign, and submit.

🗂️

Getting ready to bid

Help with SAM registration, California Small Business or DVBE certification paperwork, and a capability statement. Decisions on registration or certification are made by the issuing agency, not by us.
